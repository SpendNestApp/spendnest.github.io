Privacy Policy for SpendNest – Offline Expense Tracker

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

SpendNest is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your financial data, expenses, categories, budgets, and receipts are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store your personal financial information on our servers.

Receipt scanning is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device technologies. Your receipt images and extracted text never leave your device.

The free version of SpendNest displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect advertising-related information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

SpendNest offers optional Premium features through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Purchase information is processed securely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use SpendNest.

If you choose to back up your device using Apple's iCloud Backup feature, your app data may be included in your device backup according to your Apple settings. SpendNest does not directly upload or sync your data to any cloud service.
