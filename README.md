# SpendNest – Offline Expense Tracker

Take control of your personal finances with a beautiful expense tracker that works **completely offline**.

**No account. No cloud. No tracking.**

Everything stays on your device, giving you a fast, private, and secure way to track expenses, monitor spending, and build better financial habits. Whether you're logging your daily coffee, managing monthly budgets, or analyzing spending trends, SpendNest keeps your financial data exactly where it belongs—on your iPhone.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **100% Offline:** No internet connection required. Your data never leaves your device.
* **Privacy First:** No accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or tracking.
* **Quick Expense Entry:** Log expenses in seconds with a clean and intuitive interface.
* **On-Device Receipt Scanning:** Scan receipts using Apple's on-device OCR—no uploads or cloud processing.
* **Siri Shortcuts:** Add expenses with your voice for even faster tracking.
* **Home & Lock Screen Widgets:** View spending summaries and quickly access the app.
* **Spending Heatmap:** Visualize your daily spending patterns with a beautiful calendar-style heatmap.
* **Smart Insights:** Track spending by category, monitor monthly trends, and understand where your money goes.
* **Native iOS Experience:** Designed to feel fast, smooth, and right at home on your iPhone.
